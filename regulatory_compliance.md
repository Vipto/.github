# Regulatory Compliance Overview

**Document Type:** Legal and Organizational Disclosure
**Applies To:** Regulatory frameworks potentially relevant to Vipto's activities

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a general, honest overview of the regulatory frameworks that may be relevant to Vipto's activities as a technology product initiative operating in India, without asserting that Vipto currently holds any specific license, registration, or compliance certification that it does not actually hold. It is intended to demonstrate awareness of the regulatory landscape, not to claim completed compliance across every possible framework.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to regulatory considerations relevant to Vipto's product (a hyperlocal product discovery platform connecting shoppers with local stores), its internship program, and its general operations. It does not address tax matters, which are covered in `tax_and_financial_status.md`, or data protection matters, which are covered in `data_protection_compliance.md`.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Intermediary** | A person who, on behalf of another, receives, stores, or transmits electronic records, as defined under the Information Technology Act, 2000. |
| **E-Commerce Entity** | An entity that owns, operates, or manages a digital or electronic facility for electronic commerce, as defined under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. |

## 4. General Approach

Vipto's approach to regulatory compliance is to identify which frameworks are plausibly relevant to its actual activities, disclose that assessment honestly, and seek professional regulatory advice as its product functionality and commercial activity evolve, rather than asserting blanket or unverified compliance across all conceivable frameworks.

## 5. Information Technology Act, 2000

To the extent Vipto's application allows users to post or exchange information — for example, product availability information submitted by local businesses — Vipto's application may function, in part, as an intermediary under the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. Where this is the case, obligations such as maintaining a grievance redressal mechanism and publishing terms of use may become relevant. This document does not assert that Vipto has completed formal compliance with intermediary obligations as of its effective date; this is an area for ongoing assessment as the product's functionality develops.

## 6. Consumer Protection Framework

If and to the extent Vipto's platform facilitates transactions or connects consumers with sellers in a manner that brings it within the definition of an e-commerce entity under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, obligations such as disclosure of seller information, grievance redressal, and fair trade practices may become relevant. As of the effective date of this document, Vipto's stated function is product discovery — connecting shoppers with information about local store inventory — rather than directly facilitating transactions, and this document does not assert that e-commerce entity obligations currently apply; this classification should be reassessed if and when transactional functionality is introduced.

## 7. Shops and Establishments Legislation

State-level Shops and Establishments Acts may be relevant to a physical place of business, where one exists, and to certain employment-related obligations. As Vipto does not currently operate from a registered physical establishment in the sense contemplated by this legislation, and does not represent its interns as formal employees, this document does not assert current registration under any Shops and Establishments Act; applicability should be reassessed if Vipto's operational structure changes.

## 8. Sector-Specific Licensing

Because Vipto's platform relates to product discovery involving local retail businesses, this document notes for completeness that certain categories of goods (for example, food products) can attract sector-specific regulatory frameworks, such as the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**, administered by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India, if Vipto's platform were to directly deal in the sale or distribution of such goods rather than merely displaying third-party product availability. As of the effective date of this document, Vipto does not itself sell or distribute physical goods, and no sector-specific license is claimed; this should be reassessed if the product's functionality changes.

## 9. Foreign Exchange and Investment Regulations

Should Vipto receive foreign investment or engage in cross-border transactions in the future, frameworks such as the **Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999**, and associated regulations may become relevant. As of the effective date of this document, no foreign investment or cross-border commercial arrangement is claimed, and this document does not assert compliance with any such framework.

## 10. Labour and Employment-Related Regulation

To the extent Vipto's internship arrangements are found, on their specific facts, to resemble an employment relationship rather than a genuine internship, applicable labour welfare legislation could become relevant. Vipto's current position, as described in `internship.md` and `roles.md`, is that its interns are not represented as employees; this classification depends on the actual facts of each engagement and is not resolved definitively by this document alone.

## 11. What This Document Does Not Claim

This document does not claim that Vipto currently holds any specific government license, registration, or regulatory approval, including but not limited to Startup India recognition, an intermediary compliance certification, an FSSAI license, or any state-level shop registration, unless separately and explicitly confirmed elsewhere.

## 12. Responsibilities

The Owner is responsible for reassessing this regulatory overview as Vipto's product functionality, transaction model, and operational footprint evolve, and for seeking specific regulatory or legal advice before introducing functionality that would bring a new regulatory framework into play, such as direct transaction facilitation.

## 13. Limitations of This Document

This document is a general overview and does not constitute a regulatory compliance audit or legal opinion on Vipto's obligations under any specific framework. Regulatory applicability depends on granular facts about Vipto's actual functionality, scale, and business model, which should be assessed by a qualified legal professional, particularly before any regulatory filing, launch of new functionality, or fundraising activity.
