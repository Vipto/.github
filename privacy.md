# Privacy and Information Handling

**Last Updated:** August 2026
**Privacy Contact:** vipto.app@gmail.com

## 1. Purpose

This document describes, to the extent an actual practice currently exists, how Vipto collects, uses, and protects information relating to users, contributors, and interns. It is limited strictly to Vipto's actual current practices and does not claim certifications, registrations, or compliance frameworks that Vipto does not presently hold.

## 2. Information Collected

Depending on the nature of a person's interaction with Vipto, the following categories of information may be collected: GitHub usernames and associated public profile information, for the purpose of granting and managing repository access; email addresses and other contact information, provided during application, onboarding, or ongoing communication; internship records, including selection assessments, supervision notes, evaluation records, and completion status; and assessment or interview information provided by candidates during the internship selection process.

## 3. Purpose of Collection

Information is collected only for purposes directly connected to Vipto's operation, including granting appropriate access to development and design tools, administering the internship program and evaluating candidates and interns, maintaining accurate internal records to support internship documentation and certificate issuance, and communicating with contributors, interns, and applicants regarding their engagement with Vipto.

## 4. Who Can Access Information

Access to personal information collected by Vipto is limited to individuals who require it for the purposes described in Section 3 — principally, those responsible for internship administration, engineering supervision, and organizational record-keeping. Access is not granted broadly across all contributors or interns by default.

## 5. Access Limitations

Individuals granted access to personal information are expected to use it solely for the purpose for which it was collected and are not authorized to share it outside Vipto's internal record-keeping without a legitimate organizational reason or the consent of the individual concerned, except where disclosure is required by applicable law.

## 6. Storage Practices

Information collected by Vipto is currently stored using the platforms Vipto actively uses for its operations, including its documentation and communication systems. Vipto does not currently operate a dedicated, independently certified data storage infrastructure beyond these platforms, and this document does not claim otherwise.

## 7. Retention Practices

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this document, including maintaining a sufficient record to support future verification of internship completion or certificate authenticity. Vipto has not, as of the date of this document, adopted a fixed retention schedule beyond what is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

## 8. Sharing With Third Parties

Vipto does not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with a third party only where the individual concerned has requested or consented to such sharing — for example, in response to a legitimate verification request from a prospective employer or educational institution — or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

## 9. Security Precautions

Vipto takes reasonable precautions to protect information under its control, consistent with the practices described in [`security.md`](./security.md), including limiting access to authorized individuals and avoiding storage of sensitive credentials or personal information in insecure or publicly accessible locations, such as public repositories.

## 10. Correction and Deletion

An individual who believes information held by Vipto about them is inaccurate, or who wishes to request deletion of their information, may contact vipto.app@gmail.com. Vipto will address such requests to the extent reasonably possible, taking into account any legitimate need to retain records for verification or legal purposes.

## 11. Applicable Indian Data Protection Requirements

Vipto's handling of personal information is intended to be conducted consistently with applicable Indian data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to the extent that Act applies to Vipto's activities. This document does not constitute a formal compliance certification, and Vipto's specific obligations under that Act, or any other applicable law, depend on the nature and scale of processing actually undertaken and should be assessed independently as Vipto's operations evolve.

## 12. No Claimed Certifications

Vipto does not claim ISO certification, SOC 2 compliance, formal data protection registration, or any other certification or compliance status that it does not actually hold. Any statement to the contrary appearing outside this document should not be relied upon.

## 13. Contact

Questions or concerns regarding this document, or regarding Vipto's handling of personal information generally, should be directed to vipto.app@gmail.com.
