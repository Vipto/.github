# Data Protection and Information Security Compliance

**Document Type:** Legal and Organizational Disclosure
**Applies To:** Personal data and information collected or processed in connection with Vipto

## 1. Purpose

This document explains Vipto's current position regarding data protection and information security compliance under Indian law. It is limited strictly to Vipto's actual current practices and does not claim certifications, registrations, or a level of compliance maturity that Vipto does not presently hold.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to personal data collected from users of Vipto's application, interns, contributors, and any other individual whose information Vipto processes in connection with its operations. It supplements, and should be read together with, `privacy.md`.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Personal Data** | Data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, consistent with the definition under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. |
| **Data Fiduciary** | A person who alone or in conjunction with others determines the purpose and means of processing personal data, as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. |
| **Data Principal** | The individual to whom the personal data relates. |
| **Processing** | Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, and disclosure. |

## 4. Applicable Legal Frameworks

Vipto's handling of personal data is potentially governed by:

- **The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**, which governs the processing of digital personal data in India and imposes obligations on entities acting as Data Fiduciaries.
- **The Information Technology Act, 2000**, and in particular Section 43A and the associated Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information Rules, 2011, which impose obligations regarding the security of sensitive personal data handled by a body corporate.
- **Section 72A of the Information Technology Act, 2000**, which addresses unauthorized disclosure of information.

This document does not assert that every provision of these frameworks currently applies to Vipto in every respect; applicability depends on the nature, scale, and purpose of Vipto's actual data processing activity, which may evolve as Vipto's product and user base grow. Vipto's obligations under these frameworks should be reassessed periodically as its processing activity scales.

## 5. Data Currently Collected

Consistent with `privacy.md`, Vipto currently collects limited categories of personal data, including contact information provided by applicants, interns, and contributors, GitHub account information necessary for repository access, and information provided by users of Vipto's application to the extent the application requires it to function, such as location information relevant to hyperlocal product discovery.

## 6. Vipto's Role Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

To the extent Vipto determines the purpose and means of processing personal data collected through its application or its internship program, Vipto functions, in substance, as a Data Fiduciary under the Act. This document does not assert that Vipto has completed any formal registration or certification process under the Act; as of the effective date of this document, no such registration is claimed, and Vipto's obligations are addressed through the practical measures described in `privacy.md` and this document, pending fuller compliance review as Vipto scales.

## 7. Data Principal Rights

Consistent with the principles underlying the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, an individual whose personal data is processed by Vipto may request information regarding, correction of, or deletion of their personal data, by contacting **vipto.app@gmail.com**, as described in `privacy.md`. Vipto will address such requests to the extent reasonably possible given its current operational scale.

## 8. Security Measures

Vipto takes reasonable precautions to protect personal data under its control, including limiting access to information to individuals who require it for a legitimate purpose, avoiding storage of credentials or sensitive personal data in insecure or publicly accessible locations such as public repositories, and using established, reputable third-party platforms (such as Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, and Atlassian Confluence) for storage and communication, relying in part on the security measures those platforms provide.

This document does not claim that Vipto has independently implemented, or been certified against, any specific security standard such as ISO/IEC 27001, or that it has conducted a formal security audit, unless such a certification or audit has actually and separately taken place.

## 9. Cross-Border Data Transfer

Where personal data is processed using platforms with servers or infrastructure located outside India, such transfer is addressed to the extent applicable under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and any rules issued under it. This document does not make specific representations regarding the geographic location of data processed by third-party platforms Vipto uses, and such details, where material, should be confirmed directly with the relevant platform provider.

## 10. Data Breach Response

In the event of a suspected data breach affecting personal data under Vipto's control, Vipto will follow the incident response process described in `incident-response.md` and `security.md`, and will assess any notification obligations that may arise under applicable law based on the nature and scale of the breach.

## 11. No Claimed Certifications

Vipto does not claim ISO 27001 certification, SOC 2 compliance, formal registration as a Significant Data Fiduciary, or any other data protection certification or registration that it does not actually hold. Any statement suggesting otherwise, appearing outside this document, should not be relied upon.

## 12. Responsibilities

The Owner is responsible for ensuring that Vipto's data handling practices are reviewed periodically as its user base and processing activity grow, and for seeking professional compliance advice at the point Vipto's scale of data processing warrants a more formal compliance program.

## 13. Limitations of This Document

This document reflects Vipto's current practices and understanding of applicable law and does not constitute a formal compliance certification or legal opinion. Data protection law, particularly under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, continues to develop through subordinate rules and regulatory guidance, and Vipto's specific obligations should be reassessed as such guidance is issued and as Vipto's operations scale.

## 14. Verification

Questions regarding Vipto's data protection practices may be directed to **vipto.app@gmail.com**.
