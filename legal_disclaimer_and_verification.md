It is not prepared by, and does not carry the endorsement of, a licensed legal or financial professional acting for the reader.

## 5. No Creation of Legal Status Through Publication

The publication of any Vipto Documentation, including on GitHub or Confluence, does not itself create, confer, or evidence incorporation, legal personality, ownership, licensing, regulatory approval, contractual obligation, or employment relationship. Legal status and obligations are created, where applicable, only through the underlying facts, agreements, and legal processes described within each document — not by the existence of the document itself.

## 6. Distinguishing Fact From Assumption

Vipto Documentation aims to state clearly what is factually established (for example, that Vipto is not currently incorporated) as distinct from what is proposed, intended, or subject to future development (for example, a future intention to formalize a compliance program as Vipto scales). Readers should not treat a stated intention or aspiration as equivalent to an established fact, and Vipto Documentation seeks to avoid conflating the two.

## 7. No Fabricated Credentials

Vipto Documentation does not assert, and should never be understood to assert, the existence of any registration number, license, certification, or credential — including a CIN, LLPIN, GSTIN, PAN specific to a business entity, trademark registration number, ISO certification, or government recognition — unless that credential has been actually and separately obtained and is explicitly identified as such in the relevant document. The absence of such an assertion should be read as the absence of that credential, not as an oversight.
