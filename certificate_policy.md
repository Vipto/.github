# Certificate Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document explains precisely how Vipto handles the issuance, verification, and correction of internship-related documentation. It is intended to give interns, employers, and educational institutions a clear and accurate understanding of what a Vipto-issued certificate represents, and what it does not.

## 2. Types of Documents Vipto May Issue

Depending on eligibility and internal approval, Vipto may issue: an internship confirmation letter, confirming that an individual was engaged for an internship with Vipto; an internship completion certificate, confirming that an individual completed the stated internship period and fulfilled the stated requirements; experience or participation documentation, describing the individual's actual role, duration, and contribution; and, at the discretion of the authorized representative, a recommendation letter reflecting the individual's actual performance.

## 3. Eligibility Requirements

Eligibility for internship completion documentation requires that the intern has completed the agreed internship duration, or an early conclusion mutually acknowledged as satisfactory, met the evaluation standards described in [`internship.md`](./internship.md) and [`internship-policy.md`](./internship-policy.md), and maintained conduct consistent with Vipto's Code of Conduct throughout the engagement.

## 4. Required Internship Records

Before any certificate is issued, Vipto's internal records for the relevant intern are reviewed, including selection and onboarding records, supervision and evaluation notes maintained throughout the internship, and GitHub activity reflecting the intern's actual contribution.

## 5. Minimum Completion Requirements

An intern must have engaged meaningfully with their assigned responsibilities for a sufficient portion of the internship period to support an accurate certificate. Vipto does not issue completion certificates for engagements that did not, in substance, involve meaningful participation, regardless of the nominal duration for which the intern was notionally engaged.

## 6. Approval Authority

Certificates and internship documentation are approved and issued by Vipto's authorized representative(s) responsible for internship administration, based on the records described in Section 4, and are not issued automatically upon request without that underlying review.

## 7. Certificate Contents

A Vipto-issued certificate states the intern's full name, their role during the internship, the actual internship period, the name of the authorized representative or supervising function issuing the certificate, and Vipto's official verification contact.

## 8. Verification Contact

Every certificate issued by Vipto directs any party seeking to verify its authenticity to Vipto's sole official contact address, vipto.app@gmail.com, as described further in [`verification.md`](./verification.md).

## 9. Reference Numbers

Where implemented, a Vipto-issued certificate carries a unique reference number that can be quoted when requesting verification, allowing Vipto to confirm the specific document being referenced against its internal records.

## 10. Verification Procedure

A party seeking to verify a certificate should contact vipto.app@gmail.com with the intern's name, the internship period stated on the certificate, and, where available, the certificate's reference number. Vipto will confirm or deny the authenticity of the certificate based on its internal records.

## 11. Correction Procedure

Where an intern identifies an error in an issued certificate — for example, an incorrect date or role description — the intern should contact vipto.app@gmail.com to request a correction. Vipto will review the request against its internal records and issue a corrected document where the error is confirmed.

## 12. Revocation or Cancellation

Vipto reserves the right to revoke or cancel a previously issued certificate where it is subsequently established that the certificate was issued in error, was based on inaccurate information, or related to an internship subsequently found to have involved conduct inconsistent with its terms. Any such revocation will be communicated to the individual concerned and reflected in Vipto's internal records.

## 13. What a Vipto Certificate Does Not Represent

A certificate issued by Vipto is Vipto's own organizational documentation. It does not constitute, and must not be represented as, government accreditation, university or UGC/AICTE recognition, a formal educational qualification, or evidence of employment status or any statutory position. Any party relying on a Vipto certificate should understand it as a factual record of an internship engagement, verifiable through Vipto's official contact channel, and nothing more.

## 14. Contact

Questions regarding this policy, or any specific certificate issued by Vipto, should be directed to vipto.app@gmail.com.
