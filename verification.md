# Verification

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how any claim relating to Vipto — including its official channels, an individual's association with Vipto, or the authenticity of a Vipto-issued certificate — can be independently verified, so that third parties are not left to rely on unofficial sources.

## 2. Verifying Vipto's Official GitHub Organization

Vipto's official, authoritative GitHub Organization is located at:

**https://github.com/Vipto**

Any repository, profile, or fork claiming affiliation with Vipto outside of this organization should not be treated as officially recognized unless independently confirmed through Vipto's official contact channel.

## 3. Verifying Vipto's Official LinkedIn Presence

Vipto's official LinkedIn presence is linked from Vipto's official GitHub Organization profile and from Vipto's own public communications. A LinkedIn page or profile claiming to represent Vipto should be cross-checked against these official links before being relied upon.

## 4. Official Email

**vipto.app@gmail.com**

This is Vipto's sole official contact address, used for all verification requests described in this document, as well as for general inquiries as described in [`contact.md`](./contact.md).

## 5. Verifying an Intern's Association With Vipto

An employer, educational institution, or other interested party seeking to confirm that a named individual is or was an intern with Vipto should contact vipto.app@gmail.com, providing the individual's full name and the approximate period of the claimed internship. Vipto will confirm or clarify the individual's association with Vipto based on its internal records, subject to appropriate privacy considerations as described in [`privacy.md`](./privacy.md).

## 6. Verifying an Internship Certificate

A party holding, or presented with, a certificate purporting to be issued by Vipto may verify its authenticity by contacting vipto.app@gmail.com with the details described in [`certificate-policy.md`](./certificate-policy.md), including the certificate holder's name, the stated internship period, and any reference number appearing on the document.

## 7. Information Required for Verification

To process a verification request efficiently, Vipto asks that the requester provide the full name of the individual concerned, the specific claim being verified, any supporting document or reference number available, and the requester's own name and affiliation, particularly where the request comes from a third party such as a prospective employer.

## 8. Authorized Verification Contact

Only responses received directly from vipto.app@gmail.com should be treated as an authoritative verification. Vipto does not authorize any other individual or channel to confirm official records, certificates, or internship status on its behalf.

## 9. Expected Response Process

Vipto will make reasonable efforts to respond to legitimate verification requests in a timely manner, taking into account the volume of requests received and the need to confirm details against internal records before responding.

## 10. Certificate and Reference Numbers

Where a Vipto-issued certificate includes a unique reference number, quoting that number in a verification request allows for faster and more precise confirmation, as described in [`certificate-policy.md`](./certificate-policy.md).

## 11. What Vipto Can and Cannot Verify

Vipto can confirm factual matters within its own records, such as whether a named individual was engaged as an intern, the period of that engagement, and whether a specific certificate matches its internal records. Vipto cannot, and does not attempt to, verify matters outside its own records, such as an individual's broader employment history or claims made about Vipto by third parties without reference to a specific, checkable document.

## 12. Protection of Personal Information

In responding to verification requests, Vipto will disclose only the information reasonably necessary to confirm or deny the specific claim being verified, consistent with the privacy principles described in [`privacy.md`](./privacy.md), and will not disclose unrelated personal information about the individual concerned.

## 13. Caution Against Reliance on Unofficial Sources

Screenshots, forwarded messages, and claims made on social media regarding a person's association with Vipto, or regarding Vipto's own status, should not be relied upon as verification. Any party requiring certainty on such a matter should always confirm directly through vipto.app@gmail.com before relying on the claim.
