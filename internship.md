# Vipto Software Engineering Internship

## 1. Overview

Vipto operates a structured **Software Engineering Internship** program intended to give students practical, supervised experience contributing to a real, actively developed product. The internship is designed around genuine engineering work, structured mentorship, and a defined evaluation process, rather than passive observation or coursework simulation.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of the internship is twofold: to give interns meaningful exposure to real-world software engineering practices, including version control, code review, testing, and collaborative development, and to support Vipto's ongoing product development through supervised, structured intern contribution. The internship is treated as a genuine two-way engagement, not a formality.

## 3. Selection Process

Interns are selected through a defined process consisting of an initial assessment of relevant technical or design ability, followed by an interview to evaluate fit, communication, and commitment. Selection decisions are made by Vipto's internal leadership and are based on the candidate's demonstrated ability and availability to meet the internship's expectations.

## 4. Duration

The standard duration of the Software Engineering Internship is **three months**. Extensions, early conclusions, or adjustments to this duration may occur where circumstances warrant, and any such change is documented at the time it occurs.

## 5. Supervision

Each intern engaged under this program is supervised by Vipto's Engineering Lead who is responsible for assigning technical work, reviewing contributions, providing guidance, and evaluating the intern's progress and development throughout the internship period.

## 6. Intern Responsibilities

Interns are expected to take ownership of assigned development tasks, participate actively in Vipto's engineering workflow, communicate clearly and promptly regarding progress and blockers, and engage constructively with code review and feedback. Interns are expected to treat their assigned work with the same diligence expected of any contributor to a live, functioning product.

## 7. Onboarding

On the first day of the internship, interns are onboarded to Vipto's core tools and systems, including GitHub account setup and repository access, Microsoft Teams for internal communication, and Confluence for documentation and process reference. Interns are expected to complete this onboarding before beginning assigned development work.

## 8. GitHub Learning and Workflow

Interns are required to become proficient in Vipto's GitHub-based development workflow, including working with GitHub Issues and GitHub Projects for task tracking, creating and managing branches according to Vipto's naming conventions, opening Pull Requests for review, and responding to code review feedback in a timely and professional manner. Interns unfamiliar with these tools are provided onboarding material to reach working proficiency before being assigned independent tasks.

## 9. Communication Expectations

Microsoft Teams is the primary channel for day-to-day communication, questions, and status updates. Confluence is used for documentation, process reference, and structured knowledge that interns are expected to consult and contribute to as relevant. Interns are expected to remain responsive during their agreed working hours and to communicate proactively when blocked or unable to meet a commitment.

## 10. Sunday Build Days

Vipto operates a recurring **Sunday Build Day**, a structured, synchronous working session in which the engineering team, including interns, collaborates on active development tasks, reviews progress, and addresses blockers collectively. Attendance and active participation in Sunday Build Days is a core expectation of the internship.

## 11. Academic Commitments

Vipto recognizes that interns are, in the ordinary course, second-year and third-year students with ongoing academic obligations. The internship is structured to accommodate reasonable academic commitments, provided interns communicate scheduling conflicts in advance and continue to meet their assigned responsibilities within agreed timelines.

## 12. Evaluation

Intern performance is evaluated on an ongoing basis across five dimensions: quality and consistency of technical contribution, demonstrated learning and improvement over the internship period, reliability in meeting commitments and deadlines, clarity and professionalism of communication, and overall technical development relative to the intern's starting point.

## 13. Completion Requirements

To be considered to have successfully completed the internship, an intern must fulfil the assigned duration, meet the evaluation standards set out above, and maintain professional conduct consistent with Vipto's Code of Conduct throughout the engagement.

## 14. Certificate and Documentation Eligibility

Interns who successfully complete the internship in accordance with Section 13 are eligible to receive internship completion documentation, as described in [`certificate-policy.md`](./certificate-policy.md). Documentation eligibility is assessed against Vipto's internal records of the intern's actual engagement, contribution, and conduct.

## 15. Verification

Any claim regarding participation in the Vipto internship program may be verified through Vipto's official contact channel, vipto.app@gmail.com, as further described in [`verification.md`](./verification.md).
