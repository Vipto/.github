# Vipto

### Building technology that makes everyday product discovery simpler.

Vipto is a technology product initiative focused on solving practical problems around how people discover, explore, and connect with products and services.

We are building Vipto as a real-world product through continuous experimentation, engineering, design, and user feedback.

## What We're Building

Vipto aims to create a better and more intuitive product discovery experience by bringing useful information, discovery, and interaction into one platform.

We are currently developing and improving the product with a growing team of engineers, designers, and contributors.

## Our Focus

- Product Discovery
- Mobile & Web Technology
- User Experience
- Software Engineering
- Product Innovation

## Join Us

We are always interested in people who enjoy building, experimenting, and solving real-world problems.

Vipto also provides structured internship opportunities where students can gain practical experience by contributing to real product development.
