# Grievance Redressal and Complaint Policy

**Document Type:** Public Service and Legal Communication Policy
**Applies To:** Any person who has experienced a problem, concern, dispute, or grievance in connection with Vipto, its application, its internship program, its documentation, or any individual associated with it

## 1. Purpose

This document exists to give any person — whether a user of Vipto's application, a current or former intern, a contributor, a business partner, or a member of the public — a clear, accessible, and good-faith process for raising a problem, complaint, or legal concern relating to Vipto. Vipto recognizes that any active, growing initiative may, from time to time, give rise to a genuine grievance, misunderstanding, service issue, or legal question, and this document sets out exactly how such a matter should be raised, what response the person raising it can expect, and how Vipto approaches resolution.

This document also sets out Vipto's expectation that any concern be raised directly and in good faith through its official channel in the first instance, rather than through public statements that could unfairly damage Vipto's reputation before Vipto has had a genuine opportunity to understand and address the matter. This expectation is stated as a request grounded in fairness, not as a restriction on any person's legal rights, which are expressly preserved in Section 14 of this document.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to any complaint, dispute, or concern relating to:

1. Vipto's application, including its functionality, performance, or content;
2. Vipto's internship program, including the conduct of any supervisor, the terms of an internship, or a certificate or document issued by Vipto;
3. Any interaction with a person representing Vipto, whether an intern, contributor, or the Owner;
4. Vipto's public representations, including any statement made in its documentation, on its GitHub Organization, or on its LinkedIn page;
5. Any legal matter connected to Vipto, including a contractual, intellectual property, data protection, or consumer-related concern; and
6. Any other service-related difficulty a person has encountered in dealing with Vipto.

This document does not itself create new legal rights or obligations. It describes the process Vipto follows to receive, consider, and respond to concerns, and should be read together with `legal_disclaimer_and_verification.md` and the other governance documents referenced throughout.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Complainant** | Any individual raising a concern, complaint, or grievance under this policy. |
| **Grievance** | A specific, identifiable problem, concern, or dispute relating to Vipto, as described in Section 2. |
| **Resolution** | A response from Vipto addressing the substance of a Grievance, which may include an explanation, a correction, a remedy, or a decision that no further action is warranted, communicated to the Complainant. |
| **Official Channel** | Vipto's sole designated contact address for all communications under this policy: **vipto.app@gmail.com**. |

## 4. Our Commitment

Vipto is committed to taking every genuine concern seriously, responding to every good-faith communication it receives, treating every Complainant with respect regardless of the nature or outcome of their concern, and being transparent about what Vipto can and cannot resolve, given its current organizational scale as an unincorporated initiative, as described in `legal_entity_status.md`.

Vipto does not claim to operate a large-scale, fully staffed customer service or legal department. What Vipto commits to is a genuine, personally attentive process, given its current size, in which every communication reaches a real person responsible for addressing it.

## 5. How to Raise a Concern

Any person who has experienced a problem with Vipto — whether relating to the application, the internship program, a certificate, a contractual matter, or any other concern within the scope of this document — is asked to raise that concern directly with Vipto by writing to:

**vipto.app@gmail.com**

This is Vipto's sole official channel for receiving and resolving grievances, consistent with `contact.md` and `verification.md`. A concern raised through this channel will reach Vipto's Owner or another individual specifically authorized to handle it.

## 6. What to Include in a Complaint

To allow Vipto to understand and address a concern efficiently, a Complainant is encouraged to include:

1. Their full name and, where relevant, their role or relationship to Vipto (for example, user, intern, contributor, or business contact);
2. A clear description of the problem or concern, including what happened, when, and, where applicable, who was involved;
3. Any supporting documentation, such as screenshots, correspondence, or a certificate reference number, where relevant to the concern;
4. The outcome or resolution the Complainant is seeking; and
5. Preferred contact details for Vipto's response, if different from the email address used to send the complaint.

A Complainant is not required to have all of this information to raise a concern. Vipto will work with any Complainant to clarify the details of their concern where necessary.

## 7. Acknowledgement and Response

Vipto will make reasonable efforts to acknowledge receipt of every complaint or concern raised through the Official Channel and will review and respond to the substance of the matter as promptly as its current operational capacity allows. **We will review your communication carefully and reply to you as soon as we reasonably can.** While Vipto is not, at its current stage, in a position to guarantee a fixed response time applicable to every type of matter, Vipto treats every complaint as a priority and aims to provide a substantive response without unnecessary delay, with more urgent matters — including safety concerns, active disputes, or matters with a pressing deadline — given expedited attention.

## 8. Categories of Concerns and How They Are Handled

### 8.1 Application and Service-Related Concerns

Concerns relating to the functioning of Vipto's application — including bugs, unexpected behavior, or a poor user experience — are reviewed by Vipto's engineering function and, where appropriate, tracked and resolved in accordance with the process described in `incident-response.md` and `workflow.md`. The Complainant will be informed of the outcome once the matter has been reviewed.

### 8.2 Internship-Related Concerns

Concerns relating to an internship — including the conduct of a supervisor, the terms of an engagement, or an evaluation outcome — are addressed in accordance with the escalation process described in `internship-policy.md` and `code-of-conduct.md`. Every intern is entitled to raise such a concern without fear of retaliation, consistent with the protections described in `code-of-conduct.md`.

### 8.3 Certificate and Documentation Concerns

Concerns relating to the accuracy or authenticity of a certificate or other document issued by Vipto are handled in accordance with the correction procedure described in `certificate-policy.md`. Vipto will review its internal records and issue a correction where an error is confirmed.

### 8.4 Legal and Contractual Concerns

Concerns of a legal nature — including a dispute regarding a contract, a claim relating to intellectual property, or a data protection concern — are reviewed with particular care, given their potential significance, and Vipto may, where appropriate, seek its own professional legal advice in formulating a response. Vipto's position on the underlying legal framework relevant to such matters is set out in `contracting_authority.md`, `intellectual_property_ownership.md`, and `data_protection_compliance.md`, as applicable.

### 8.5 Data Protection Concerns

A concern relating to the handling of personal data, including a request to access, correct, or delete personal data, is handled in accordance with `privacy.md` and `data_protection_compliance.md`.

### 8.6 General Service or Reputational Concerns

Any other concern not falling neatly within the categories above — including general feedback, a service-related complaint, or a concern about how Vipto or an individual associated with it has been represented — is reviewed by Vipto's Owner directly.

## 9. Escalation Within Vipto

Where an initial response to a Complainant does not resolve the matter to their satisfaction, the Complainant may request that the matter be escalated for further review by replying to Vipto's initial response and explaining why the concern remains unresolved. Vipto's Owner holds final responsibility for the resolution of any escalated matter, consistent with the authority described in `founder_authority.md`.

## 10. Confidentiality of Complaints

Vipto will handle information provided in connection with a complaint with appropriate discretion, disclosing it internally only to the individuals who reasonably need to know it in order to investigate and resolve the matter, consistent with the principles described in `privacy.md`.

## 11. Requesting Fair and Direct Communication Before Public Escalation

Vipto asks that any person with a genuine concern raise it first, and directly, through the Official Channel described in this document, before escalating that concern to a public forum such as social media, a public review platform, or a public GitHub Issue. This request is made for the following reasons:

1. A direct communication allows Vipto to understand the full facts of a concern, which may not be apparent from a public post alone, and to correct a genuine problem promptly;
2. Vipto is a small, unincorporated initiative, as described in `legal_entity_status.md`, and a public allegation — even one made in good faith but based on an incomplete understanding of the facts — can cause disproportionate reputational harm relative to its actual accuracy or severity;
3. Most genuine concerns can be resolved satisfactorily through direct communication, without the need for public escalation.

Vipto requests, in particular, that no person post, publish, or communicate to a third party any statement about Vipto, or any individual associated with it, that is **knowingly false, deliberately misleading, or made with the intent to harass, defame, or maliciously damage Vipto's reputation or that of any individual connected with it**, rather than to genuinely resolve a concern. This request does not extend to honest, good-faith expression of a genuine experience, opinion, or review, which Vipto respects as a legitimate right of any person who has interacted with it.

## 12. What This Policy Does Not Restrict

This policy does not, and is not intended to, restrict, discourage, or penalize:

1. A person's right to leave an honest, good-faith review or account of their genuine experience with Vipto, whether positive or negative;
2. A person's right to report a matter to an appropriate regulatory authority, consumer forum, or law enforcement body;
3. A person's right to pursue a legal claim against Vipto or its Owner through appropriate legal channels;
4. A person's right to publicly discuss a matter after having given Vipto a fair and reasonable opportunity to respond through the Official Channel, where Vipto has failed to engage in good faith.

Nothing in this document should be read as an attempt to silence legitimate criticism, suppress a consumer's rights, or discourage a person from seeking appropriate legal or regulatory recourse. It is a request for fairness and direct communication, not a legal restriction on expression.

## 13. Relevant Legal Context

Vipto's approach to grievance handling is informed by, though not necessarily governed in every particular by, principles found in the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**, which encourages fair and timely grievance redressal for consumers; the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, which addresses matters including unauthorized disclosure of information and the responsibilities of platforms handling user content; and general principles of **defamation law** under Indian civil and criminal law, which protect both a person's right to fair comment and an entity's or individual's right not to be subjected to knowingly false and damaging statements. This document does not assert that Vipto is currently subject to every formal obligation arising under these frameworks, such as a statutory requirement to appoint a designated Grievance Officer, which typically applies to entities of a certain scale or category; Vipto nonetheless follows the spirit of fair, responsive grievance handling described in this document as a matter of good practice.

## 14. Preservation of Legal Rights

Nothing in this document limits, waives, or restricts any legal right that a Complainant may hold under applicable Indian law, including the right to pursue a claim before a consumer forum, a civil court, a regulatory authority, or any other appropriate body. This document describes Vipto's preferred and requested process for resolving concerns amicably and directly; it is not, and is not intended to operate as, a mandatory pre-condition to the exercise of any legal right, except to the extent any specific written agreement between Vipto and a particular individual separately and validly provides otherwise.

## 15. Our Request in Summary

If you have experienced any problem with Vipto — whether relating to our application, our internship program, a document we have issued, or any other matter — **please write to us first at vipto.app@gmail.com.** Tell us what happened. We will review it carefully and get back to you as soon as we can. We are a small, growing initiative, and we take every concern seriously. We simply ask that you give us a fair chance to understand and address the issue directly, and that you avoid making public statements intended to harm Vipto's name or reputation before doing so. We commit, in turn, to listening, responding honestly, and doing what we reasonably can to make things right.

## 16. Responsibilities

Vipto's Owner is responsible for ensuring that every complaint received through the Official Channel is reviewed and addressed in accordance with this document, and for maintaining a fair, respectful, and non-retaliatory process for every Complainant, including current and former interns and contributors.

## 17. Limitations of This Document

This document describes Vipto's internal grievance-handling process and its request for direct, good-faith communication. It does not constitute a formal statutory grievance mechanism unless one is separately and explicitly established, and it does not limit any Complainant's independent legal rights, as set out in Section 14.
