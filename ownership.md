# Ownership of Vipto

**Document Type:** Organizational and Legal Transparency Disclosure
**Applies To:** Ownership of the Vipto initiative, its brand, and its operational assets

## 1. Purpose

This document clarifies what "ownership" of Vipto currently means in practice and in law, given that Vipto is not incorporated. It distinguishes ownership of the initiative itself from ownership of specific assets, such as intellectual property, which is addressed in greater detail in `intellectual_property_ownership.md`.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to the question of who owns and controls Vipto as an initiative, its name and brand, and its operational assets generally. It does not resolve specific intellectual property ownership questions relating to individual contributions, which are governed by `intellectual_property_ownership.md`.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ownership (in this document)** | The practical and legal control over, and responsibility for, the Vipto initiative and its assets, as distinct from shareholding in an incorporated company, which does not exist here. |
| **Brand Assets** | Vipto's name, logo, and associated identifiers used in its public presence. |
| **Operational Assets** | Systems, accounts, and infrastructure used to run Vipto, including its GitHub Organization, official email account, and associated platform accounts. |

## 4. Ownership Structure

Because Vipto is not incorporated, as established in `legal_entity_status.md`, there is no share capital, no shareholding structure, and no statutory register of members or shareholders. Ownership of Vipto, in the only sense meaningfully applicable to an unincorporated initiative, rests with its Owner, **Ayush Vaidya**, who conceived of, operates, and bears responsibility for Vipto.

This form of ownership is factual and operational rather than a creature of company law. It should not be described, in any Vipto communication, using terminology such as "shareholder," "equity holder," or "majority owner" in the corporate sense, as no such structure currently exists.

## 5. Ownership of Brand Assets

The name "Vipto," and any associated logo or visual identity currently in use, are treated as belonging to and controlled by the Owner, in the absence of any separate registered trademark or design registration. This document does not assert that any trademark registration currently exists; the position regarding formal trademark protection, if pursued, would be governed by the **Trade Marks Act, 1999**, and is not addressed as an existing status in this document.

## 6. Ownership of Operational Assets

Operational assets used to run Vipto — including but not limited to its GitHub Organization, its official email account (vipto.app@gmail.com), and any associated developer or platform accounts — are controlled by the Owner, who holds the administrative credentials and ultimate authority over these systems. Individuals granted access to these systems, such as an Engineering Lead with GitHub Organization administrative rights, hold that access on a delegated and revocable basis, and such access does not itself confer any ownership interest.

## 7. Ownership of the Product

The Vipto application, including its source code, design assets, and associated intellectual property, is addressed comprehensively in `intellectual_property_ownership.md`. As a general position, work created specifically for Vipto by contributors and interns in the course of their engagement is intended to be owned and controlled by Vipto's Owner on Vipto's behalf, subject to the specific terms of each individual's engagement and the more detailed treatment in that document.

## 8. What Ownership Does Not Confer

Ownership of Vipto, as described in this document, does not itself:

1. Create a separate legal entity or incorporate Vipto;
2. Establish any statutory protection, license, or registration that has not been separately and actually obtained;
3. Automatically transfer to any other individual, including a Contributor, Intern, or Engineering Lead, by virtue of their role or contribution, absent a specific written agreement to that effect;
4. Limit the Owner's personal liability for Vipto's activities, for the reasons explained in `legal_entity_status.md`.

## 9. Future Changes in Ownership Structure

Should Vipto be incorporated in the future, ownership would be reconstituted according to the applicable corporate structure chosen (for example, shareholding in a Private Limited Company under the Companies Act, 2013), and this document would be revised accordingly to reflect the new structure, including any conversion of the Owner's current interest into formal equity or partnership interest.

## 10. Third-Party Interests

As of the effective date of this document, no third party — including any intern, contributor, investor, or advisor — holds a formal ownership interest in Vipto. Any future arrangement involving investment, equity, or a formal ownership interest granted to a third party would be documented separately and would not be implied by mere participation in Vipto's activities, contribution to its codebase, or informal discussion.

## 11. Responsibilities

The Owner is responsible for maintaining accurate records of Vipto's operational assets and for ensuring that no communication issued in Vipto's name misrepresents the ownership structure described in this document. Any individual granted administrative access to a Vipto system is responsible for using that access only within the scope for which it was granted.

## 12. Limitations of This Document

This document reflects Vipto's ownership structure as an unincorporated initiative and does not constitute a formal instrument of transfer, assignment, or corporate constitution. It should not be relied upon as a substitute for a specific written ownership or equity agreement in the event such an agreement becomes necessary, including in connection with investment or incorporation.
