# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out the standard of professional behaviour expected of everyone engaging with Vipto — including interns, contributors, engineering team members, and design collaborators — across all of Vipto's platforms, including GitHub, Microsoft Teams, and Confluence, as well as in-person or synchronous sessions such as Sunday Build Days.

## 2. Respectful Communication

All communication within Vipto's channels should be respectful, constructive, and professional in tone, regardless of the seniority or role of the individuals involved. Disagreement on technical, product, or process matters is expected and welcome, but should be expressed through reasoned discussion rather than personal criticism.

## 3. Professional Behaviour

Everyone engaging with Vipto is expected to conduct themselves professionally, including being punctual and prepared for scheduled sessions, meeting commitments made to the team, and representing Vipto appropriately in any external communication made on its behalf.

## 4. Collaboration

Vipto operates as a small, largely asynchronous team, and effective collaboration depends on individuals engaging constructively with one another's work, providing helpful and specific feedback during code and design review, and being open to receiving feedback in the same spirit.

## 5. Communication Through Teams and GitHub

Microsoft Teams and GitHub are the primary channels for Vipto's internal communication. Messages and comments on these platforms should remain on-topic, professional, and free of language that would be inappropriate in a workplace setting. Public GitHub comments, in particular, should be written with the understanding that they may be visible beyond Vipto's internal team.

## 6. Conduct During Meetings

During Sunday Build Days and any other synchronous meeting, participants are expected to engage actively, avoid disruptive behaviour, and respect the time and contributions of others. Side conversations or off-topic disruption during structured working sessions should be avoided.

## 7. Prohibition on Harassment and Discrimination

Vipto does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behaviour of any kind, whether based on gender, religion, caste, ethnicity, disability, sexual orientation, age, or any other personal characteristic. This applies equally to interactions on Vipto's platforms and to any Vipto-related interaction occurring outside them.

## 8. No Unauthorized Access

Contributors and interns must not attempt to access repositories, systems, or information beyond what has been explicitly granted to them, and must not attempt to circumvent access controls implemented by Vipto.

## 9. No Misuse of Repositories

Vipto's repositories must not be used for any purpose unrelated to Vipto's legitimate development activity, including but not limited to hosting unrelated personal projects, distributing unauthorized third-party material, or introducing malicious code of any kind.

## 10. Confidentiality Expectations

Individuals engaging with Vipto may have access to information that is not intended for public disclosure, including product plans, unreleased features, and internal process documentation. Such information should be treated as confidential and not disclosed externally without authorization, consistent with any confidentiality terms applicable to the individual's engagement.

## 11. Reporting Mechanism

Any concern regarding a violation of this Code of Conduct should be reported to Vipto's official contact address, vipto.app@gmail.com. Reports may be made by anyone engaging with Vipto, including interns, contributors, or third parties who have witnessed or experienced conduct inconsistent with this document.

## 12. Handling of Complaints

Complaints received under this Code of Conduct will be reviewed by an authorized representative of Vipto, treated with appropriate discretion, and addressed in a manner proportionate to the nature and severity of the conduct reported. Vipto will make reasonable efforts to investigate promptly and fairly.

## 13. Consequences for Violations

Depending on the nature and severity of a violation, consequences may range from an informal conversation and expectation-setting, to formal warning, to suspension of access to Vipto's systems, to termination of an internship or contributor relationship. Vipto will exercise judgment proportionate to the conduct involved.

## 14. Responsible Authority

Enforcement of this Code of Conduct is the responsibility of Vipto's authorized leadership, acting on reports received and their own observations. Where a report concerns a member of leadership, it should still be directed to the official contact address, and will be handled with appropriate independence.

## 15. Protection Against Retaliation

Vipto does not tolerate retaliation against any individual who, in good faith, reports a concern under this Code of Conduct or participates in the investigation of such a report. Any concern regarding retaliation should itself be reported through the same channel.
