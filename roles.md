# Roles and Responsibilities

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the actual working responsibilities associated with each functional role at Vipto. As set out in [`organization.md`](./organization.md), these are internal, working responsibilities, and should not be understood as statutory corporate offices or formal legal positions.

## 2. Owner

The Owner function is responsible for setting Vipto's overall product direction, including deciding what the product should become and in what sequence. This function makes final business and product decisions where consensus among the team is not reached, coordinates activity across the engineering and design functions to ensure alignment with product priorities, and makes organizational decisions regarding Vipto's structure, public representation, and strategic direction.

## 3. Engineering Lead

The Engineering Lead is responsible for providing engineering guidance across the team's technical work, coordinating the assignment and sequencing of development tasks, reviewing code submitted through Pull Requests to maintain quality and consistency, setting technical direction for the product's architecture and implementation approach, supervising Software Engineering Interns throughout their engagement, and maintaining overall responsibility for the quality of Vipto's engineering output.

## 4. Software Engineers

Software Engineers contributing to Vipto are responsible for implementing assigned features and fixes in accordance with Vipto's engineering workflow, working through GitHub Issues assigned to them, creating and managing branches consistent with Vipto's conventions, submitting Pull Requests for review, testing their own contributions prior to and during review, and maintaining documentation relevant to their work.

## 5. Software Engineering Interns

Software Engineering Interns hold the same core responsibilities as Software Engineers, adjusted for their developmental stage and the structured supervision described in [`internship.md`](./internship.md). This includes completing assigned development tasks, actively pursuing learning and skill development throughout the internship, following Vipto's GitHub-based workflow, maintaining clear and proactive communication with their supervisor, participating constructively in code review both as a recipient and, as appropriate, a contributor of feedback, carrying out testing relevant to their assigned work, and participating in Sunday Build Days as a core part of the internship structure.

## 6. UI/UX

Individuals contributing to UI/UX at Vipto are responsible for the design of Vipto's product interface in a manner consistent with the product's intended user experience, maintaining and applying Vipto's design system through Figma, collaborating closely with engineering during the implementation of designs, and preparing design handoff materials that are complete and usable enough for engineering to implement without significant ambiguity.

## 7. Working Responsibilities, Not Legal Positions

Consistent with [`organization.md`](./organization.md), the responsibilities described in this document are working responsibilities assigned for the effective operation of Vipto's product development. They do not, by themselves, establish a statutory directorship, partnership interest, formal employment relationship, or any other legally defined position. Any person's actual legal relationship with Vipto — whether as an unpaid intern, a paid contributor, or otherwise — is governed by the specific terms of their individual engagement, as documented at the time of engagement, and not by the title or role described in this document.

## 8. Escalation and Overlap

Where responsibility for a particular decision is not clearly covered by the roles described above, or where responsibilities appear to overlap, the matter should be raised with the Owner function or the Engineering Lead, as appropriate to the subject matter, for resolution, and this document should be updated to reflect the clarified position where the ambiguity is likely to recur.
