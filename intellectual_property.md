# Intellectual Property

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out how Vipto approaches intellectual property created during the course of contribution to its projects, including source code, designs, documentation, and product ideas. It reflects Vipto's current position and does not assert ownership arrangements beyond what has actually been agreed with the relevant contributor or intern.

## 2. Source Code

Source code developed by an intern or contributor specifically for, and in the course of, their engagement with Vipto is intended to be used by Vipto for the ongoing development of its product, consistent with the terms of that individual's engagement. Where an individual's specific engagement includes a written agreement addressing ownership of such code, that agreement governs; where it does not, this document reflects Vipto's general expectation, not a substitute for such an agreement.

## 3. UI/UX Designs

Designs, design system components, and related assets produced by UI/UX contributors or interns specifically for Vipto's product are intended for use by Vipto in connection with that product, on the same basis described in Section 2.

## 4. Documentation

Documentation authored by contributors or interns in the course of their engagement — including technical documentation, process documentation, and internal guides — is intended to remain with Vipto as part of its organizational knowledge base, for continued use and maintenance by the team.

## 5. Product Ideas

Product ideas and feature concepts discussed or developed within Vipto's internal channels, including during Sunday Build Days or in Microsoft Teams, are treated as contributions to Vipto's collective product development process, rather than as individually owned intellectual property, unless a specific written agreement provides otherwise.

## 6. Technical Solutions

Technical solutions, architectural decisions, and implementation approaches developed in the course of an individual's engagement with Vipto are treated in the same manner as source code under Section 2, as they are typically embodied in and inseparable from the codebase itself.

## 7. Intern Contributions

The general expectations set out in this document apply to Software Engineering Interns and UI/UX Interns in the same manner as to other contributors, subject to the specific terms of each intern's individual engagement as documented at onboarding.

## 8. Contributor Contributions

Individuals contributing to Vipto's public or open repositories are additionally subject to the licensing terms described in [`open-source.md`](./open-source.md) and [`contributing.md`](./contributing.md), which govern how their contributions may be used and distributed.

## 9. Third-Party Libraries and Open-Source Code

Any third-party library, framework, or open-source component incorporated into Vipto's product remains subject to its own license terms, and Vipto's use of such components is intended to comply with those terms. Contributors introducing a new third-party dependency should confirm its license is compatible with Vipto's intended use before incorporating it.

## 10. Licenses

Where a Vipto repository specifies a license, that license governs the terms on which the code in that repository may be used, modified, and distributed by third parties, as further described in [`open-source.md`](./open-source.md).

## 11. Confidential Information

Information that is not intended for public disclosure — including unreleased product plans, internal technical documentation, and business strategy — is treated as confidential and is addressed further in [`code-of-conduct.md`](./code-of-conduct.md) and [`privacy.md`](./privacy.md).

## 12. Pre-Existing Intellectual Property

Nothing in this document is intended to transfer ownership of intellectual property that a contributor or intern created prior to, and independently of, their engagement with Vipto. Contributors should not incorporate their own pre-existing proprietary work into a Vipto repository without first clarifying the ownership implications with the Engineering Lead.

## 13. Ownership and Usage Arrangements

Where the ownership or usage of a particular piece of work requires clarity beyond the general expectations described in this document — for example, in relation to a specific paid engagement or a significant individual contribution — that arrangement should be addressed through a specific written agreement between Vipto and the individual concerned, rather than relying on this general document alone.

## 14. Requirement for Written Agreements

Vipto's general practice, as described in this document, does not substitute for a written agreement where the value, sensitivity, or nature of a specific contribution warrants one. Contributors or interns who believe such an agreement is appropriate for their situation should raise the matter with the Engineering Lead or Vipto's official contact address.

## 15. Restrictions on Unauthorized Reuse or Disclosure

Contributors and interns must not reuse, republish, or disclose Vipto's proprietary code, designs, or documentation outside of Vipto without authorization, whether during or after their engagement, except to the extent such material is separately released under an open-source license as described in [`open-source.md`](./open-source.md).
