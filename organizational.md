# Vipto — Organizational Structure

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how Vipto is currently structured and how responsibility is distributed internally. It is written to give an accurate picture of Vipto's working structure without creating or implying corporate titles or statutory positions that do not exist.

## 2. Current Status

Vipto currently operates as an unincorporated technology product and startup initiative, as set out in full in [`legal.md`](./legal.md). Its organizational structure reflects a functional division of responsibility appropriate to a lean, actively developing product team, rather than the formal governance structure of a registered company.

## 3. Structure Overview

Vipto's current structure consists of the following functional areas:

- **Owner** — responsible for overall product direction and organizational decision-making.
- **Engineering Lead** — responsible for engineering direction, technical decision-making, and supervision of the engineering team.
- **Software Engineering Interns** — responsible for assigned development contributions under the Engineering Lead's supervision.
- **UI/UX Contributors/Interns** — responsible for interface design, design system maintenance, and design-to-development handoff.
- **Other Contributors** — individuals contributing to specific initiatives, repositories, or tasks on a defined basis, where applicable.

## 4. Owner

The Owner function is responsible for setting Vipto's overall product direction, making business and product decisions, coordinating across engineering and design functions, and making organizational decisions regarding Vipto's structure, priorities, and public representation.

## 5. Engineering Lead

The Engineering Lead holds responsibility for the technical direction of Vipto's product, coordination of engineering tasks across the team, review of code contributed by engineers and interns, supervision and evaluation of Software Engineering Interns, and overall responsibility for development quality and engineering process adherence.

## 6. Software Engineering Interns

Software Engineering Interns are responsible for completing assigned development tasks through Vipto's standard GitHub-based workflow, submitting work for review through Pull Requests, participating in testing of their own and others' contributions, and maintaining accurate documentation of their work where required.

## 7. UI/UX Contributors and Interns

UI/UX contributors and interns are responsible for the design of Vipto's product interface, maintaining consistency within Vipto's design system, collaborating with engineering during implementation, and preparing design assets for developer handoff in a usable and well-documented form.

## 8. GitHub Organization Administration

Administration of Vipto's GitHub Organization — including repository creation, access provisioning, and organizational settings — is managed by individuals specifically authorized to do so within the engineering function. Repository access is granted on a role-appropriate basis and is limited to what is necessary for a contributor's assigned responsibilities.

## 9. Task Assignment Authority

Authority to assign development or design tasks rests with the Engineering Lead for engineering work and with the relevant design lead function for UI/UX work. Task assignment is documented through GitHub Issues and Projects to maintain a transparent, auditable record of ownership.

## 10. Internship Supervision Authority

Supervision of interns — including day-to-day guidance, progress evaluation, and completion assessment — is exercised by the Engineering Lead for Software Engineering Interns, consistent with the framework set out in [`internship.md`](./internship.md).

## 11. Certificate and Document Approval Authority

Authority to approve and issue internship-related documentation, including confirmation letters and completion certificates, rests with Vipto's authorized representative(s) responsible for internship administration, acting on the basis of internal records maintained throughout the internship, as described in [`certificate-policy.md`](./certificate-policy.md).

## 12. Internal Roles Are Not Legal Titles

It is important to state clearly that the roles and titles described in this document — including "Owner," "Engineering Lead," "Intern," or "Contributor" — describe **internal, functional working responsibilities**. They are not statutory corporate offices, directorships, partnership interests, or formal employment designations under Indian company or labour law. No individual's internal title at Vipto should be relied upon as evidence of a legal or corporate position beyond the working responsibility it describes. Where a person's actual legal relationship with Vipto has independent significance — for example, for tax, immigration, or regulatory purposes — that relationship should be assessed on its own facts and, where necessary, with professional advice, rather than inferred from an internal title.
