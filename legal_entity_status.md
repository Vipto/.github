# Legal Entity Status of Vipto

**Document Type:** Legal and Organizational Disclosure
**Applies To:** Vipto, its GitHub Organization, and all associated public and internal documentation

## 1. Purpose

This document exists to state, without ambiguity, what Vipto's legal status currently is and is not, under Indian law. It is published so that no intern, contributor, employer, educational institution, business partner, or member of the public is required to infer Vipto's legal status from indirect signals such as its GitHub Organization, LinkedIn presence, or published application. This is a factual disclosure, not a legal opinion, and it does not itself create, confer, or evidence any legal status beyond what is stated here.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to Vipto as a whole, including its product development activity, its internship program, its public and private repositories, and any communication issued in Vipto's name. It governs how Vipto's legal status should be described and understood by anyone interacting with Vipto in any capacity.

For the purposes of this document and all related Vipto documentation:

## 4. Current Legal Status of Vipto

As of the effective date of this document, **Vipto is not incorporated**. Specifically, Vipto is not:

- A Private Limited Company or Public Limited Company registered under the **Companies Act, 2013**;
- A Limited Liability Partnership registered under the **Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008**;
- A Partnership Firm governed by the **Indian Partnership Act, 1932**;
- A registered society, trust, or Section 8 not-for-profit company;
- A government-recognized, government-approved, or government-affiliated body of any kind.

Vipto currently operates as an **unincorporated technology product initiative**, carried on directly by its Owner, Ayush Vaidya, without the interposition of a separate corporate legal personality.

## 5. What This Status Means in Practice

Because Vipto has no separate legal personality distinct from its Owner, the following consequences follow as a matter of ordinary legal principle, and are stated here for clarity rather than as novel claims:

1. **No Separate Liability Shield.** Vipto does not benefit from limited liability in the way an incorporated company or LLP would. Legal responsibility for activities carried out in Vipto's name rests with the individual(s) actually carrying on those activities.
2. **No Independent Contracting Capacity.** Vipto, as a name, cannot itself be a party to a contract. Any agreement entered into "with Vipto" is, as a matter of law, an agreement with the individual(s) operating Vipto, acting in that capacity, as further explained in `contracting_authority.md`.
3. **No Independent Asset Ownership.** Vipto, as a name, cannot itself own property, intellectual property, or funds. Ownership questions relating to assets used in or produced through Vipto's activities are addressed in `ownership.md` and `intellectual_property_ownership.md`.
4. **No Statutory Registrations Implied.** The absence of incorporation means Vipto does not hold, and should not be represented as holding, a Corporate Identification Number (CIN), a Limited Liability Partnership Identification Number (LLPIN), or any other registration number associated with incorporated status.

## 6. What Vipto's Operational Presence Does and Does Not Establish

Vipto maintains a genuine, active, and verifiable operational presence, including a GitHub Organization, a LinkedIn page, and a published application. This presence is real and demonstrates that Vipto is an active initiative with an identifiable body of work and ongoing accountability. However, under Indian law, none of the following establish or evidence incorporation or any specific legal entity status:

- Maintaining a GitHub Organization;
- Maintaining a LinkedIn business page;
- Publishing an application on the Google Play Store or any other application marketplace;
- Using a distinct brand name or logo;
- Engaging interns or contributors;
- Issuing internal documentation, policies, or certificates.

These activities demonstrate operational reality and, in some cases, platform-level accountability (for example, Play Store developer verification), but they are legally distinct from, and not a substitute for, incorporation under the Companies Act, 2013, or registration under any other applicable statute.
