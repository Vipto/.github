# Authorized Representatives

**Document Type:** Internal Governance and Public Verification Disclosure
**Applies To:** Individuals authorized to act, communicate, or make representations on Vipto's behalf

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies, by function rather than by unnecessary personal detail, who is authorized to act on Vipto's behalf in various capacities, and clarifies the limits of that authorization. It exists so that third parties — including interns, contributors, employers, and business contacts — can distinguish an authorized communication from an unauthorized one.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to all communications, decisions, and representations made in Vipto's name, whether directed internally to contributors and interns or externally to third parties. It does not itself grant authority; it records and discloses authority that has been assigned under `founder_authority.md` and `roles.md`.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Authorized Representative** | An individual expressly permitted to act, communicate, or make representations on Vipto's behalf within a defined scope. |
| **Apparent Authority** | The impression, whether accurate or not, that a person is authorized to act for Vipto, which this document seeks to prevent by clarifying actual authority. |
| **Functional Role** | A working responsibility within Vipto, such as Owner, Engineering Lead, or Contributor, as described in `roles.md`. |

## 4. General Principle

Authority to act on Vipto's behalf is not automatically held by every individual associated with Vipto, including every intern and contributor. Authority is specific to defined functions and is not implied by mere participation in Vipto's activities, such as having a GitHub account with repository access or being listed as a contributor to a public repository.

## 5. The Owner

Vipto's Owner, Ayush Vaidya, holds overall and ultimate authority to represent Vipto in any capacity, as described in `founder_authority.md`. The Owner is the only individual whose authority is not derived from delegation, and the Owner may exercise, delegate, or revoke authority across every functional area of Vipto's operation.

## 6. The Engineering Lead Function

The Engineering Lead function is authorized to represent Vipto specifically in relation to technical and engineering matters, including: assigning and coordinating development tasks; reviewing and approving code contributions; supervising and evaluating Software Engineering Interns; and communicating engineering-related decisions to the team. This authorization is functional and role-based; it identifies whoever currently occupies the Engineering Lead function, without naming that individual, consistent with Vipto's practice of limiting personal identification in its documentation.

The Engineering Lead function is **not** authorized to enter binding external contracts on Vipto's behalf, approve Vipto's overall public representation or legal status, or issue or approve internship completion certificates, except where expressly authorized to do so under `certificate-policy.md`.

## 7. Other Functional Roles

Other functional roles — including Product/Design leadership, Software Engineers, UI/UX Contributors, and Interns — hold authority limited strictly to their assigned working responsibilities, as described in `roles.md`. None of these roles are authorized, by virtue of the role alone, to make binding representations on Vipto's behalf to third parties, enter contracts in Vipto's name, or issue official Vipto documentation such as certificates or legal disclosures.

## 8. Table of Representative Authority

| Function | Internal Task Authority | External Representation Authority | Contracting Authority | Certificate Issuance Authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Full | Full | Yes (see `contracting_authority.md`) | Yes |
| Engineering Lead | Engineering scope only | Limited to engineering matters, as authorized | No | As specifically authorized under `certificate-policy.md` |
| Software Engineers / Interns | Assigned tasks only | No | No | No |
| UI/UX Contributors | Assigned tasks only | No | No | No |

## 9. Communications Purporting to Be From Vipto

Any communication claiming to be an official Vipto communication should originate from, or be verifiable through, Vipto's sole official contact channel, **vipto.app@gmail.com**, or from an individual whose authority for the specific matter is confirmed against this document. A message sent through informal channels such as personal social media accounts, without reference to or confirmation via the official channel, should not be treated as an authorized Vipto communication.

## 10. Limits on Apparent Authority

Vipto does not accept responsibility for representations made by individuals who are not, in fact, authorized under this document, even if such individuals hold some functional association with Vipto, such as being a current or former intern or contributor. Any third party relying on a representation of significant consequence — such as a contractual commitment or a legal claim about Vipto's status — is encouraged to verify the representative's authority through Vipto's official contact channel before relying on it.

## 11. Revocation of Authority

Authority delegated to any functional role may be revoked or modified by the Owner at any time. Where an individual's engagement with Vipto concludes, their authority to represent Vipto in any capacity ends automatically upon that conclusion, regardless of any residual access to Vipto's systems that has not yet been formally revoked.

## 12. Responsibilities

Individuals holding delegated authority are responsible for exercising it only within its defined scope, for identifying themselves accurately by function when communicating on Vipto's behalf, and for escalating any matter falling outside their authorized scope to the Owner or another appropriately authorized function.

## 13. Limitations of This Document

This document reflects Vipto's current internal allocation of authority and is subject to change as Vipto's structure evolves. It does not constitute a power of attorney or other formal legal instrument, and any third party requiring a formal, legally binding confirmation of authority for a significant transaction should request specific written confirmation from the Owner rather than relying on this document alone.

## 14. Verification

Questions regarding whether a specific individual is currently authorized to act on Vipto's behalf, in what capacity, and within what limits, may be directed to **vipto.app@gmail.com**.
