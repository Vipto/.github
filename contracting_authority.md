# Contracting Authority

**Document Type:** Legal and Organizational Disclosure
**Applies To:** The capacity to enter binding agreements in Vipto's name

## 1. Purpose

This document explains, clearly and without overstatement, who has the legal capacity to enter into binding agreements described as being "with Vipto," what that capacity legally means given Vipto's unincorporated status, and what limits apply. It is intended for interns, contributors, vendors, partners, and any other party contemplating a written or informal agreement referencing Vipto.

## 2. Scope

This document applies to any agreement, whether formal or informal, written or oral, in which Vipto is described as a contracting party — including internship agreements, vendor arrangements, contributor agreements, and any commercial or collaborative arrangement.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Contracting Party** | The legal person who actually bears the rights and obligations under an agreement. |
| **Competent Person** | A person legally capable of entering into a contract under Section 11 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872. |
| **Agreement "With Vipto"** | An agreement in which Vipto is named informally as a party, understood in law to mean an agreement with the individual(s) operating Vipto. |

## 4. Legal Basis for Contracting

The foundational law governing any contract entered into in connection with Vipto is the **Indian Contract Act, 1872**. Under Sections 10 and 11 of that Act, a contract is valid where it is entered into by competent parties — persons of the age of majority, of sound mind, and not otherwise disqualified by law — for lawful consideration and a lawful object, with free and genuine consent. Incorporation is not a precondition of contractual capacity; an individual operating an unincorporated initiative is fully capable of entering binding agreements in connection with that initiative.

## 5. Who Is the Actual Contracting Party

Because Vipto has no separate legal personality, as established in `legal_entity_status.md`, **Vipto itself cannot be a party to a contract**. Any agreement described as being "with Vipto" is, as a matter of law, an agreement with the individual who signs or otherwise enters into it on Vipto's behalf — ordinarily, the Owner, Ayush Vaidya, or another individual specifically and expressly authorized to contract on the Owner's behalf for a defined purpose, as described in `authorized_representatives.md`.

This means that rights and obligations arising under such an agreement attach, as a matter of law, to that individual personally, acting in connection with the Vipto initiative, rather than to a separate corporate entity.

## 6. Who Currently Holds Contracting Authority

| Function | Authority to Contract on Vipto's Behalf |
|---|---|
| Owner | Full authority, subject to applicable law |
| Engineering Lead | No general contracting authority; may be specifically and expressly authorized for a defined, limited purpose |
| Interns, Contributors, other functional roles | No contracting authority |

Except where the Owner has provided specific, express, written authorization for a defined purpose, no other individual associated with Vipto — including any intern, contributor, or Engineering Lead — is authorized to bind Vipto or its Owner to a contractual commitment.

## 7. Internship Agreements

Written internship communications issued by Vipto, as described in `internship.md`, constitute contracts between the intern and the Owner (acting in connection with Vipto), governed by the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and, where relevant, principles drawn from labour and employment law depending on the actual nature of the engagement. These agreements are valid and binding notwithstanding Vipto's unincorporated status, for the reasons set out in Section 4 above.

## 8. Vendor and Commercial Agreements

Any commercial agreement entered into in Vipto's name — for example, with a software vendor, a service provider, or a business partner — should be understood, and should ideally be documented, as an agreement between that counterparty and the Owner personally, in connection with the Vipto initiative, until such time as Vipto may be incorporated and a separate contracting entity established.

## 9. Limitations on Contracting Authority

The authority described in this document does not extend to:

1. Contracts requiring a form of execution only available to an incorporated entity, such as execution under a company's common seal;
2. Regulatory filings or licenses that require an incorporated applicant;
3. Any representation that a contract is entered into by "Vipto Private Limited," "Vipto LLP," or any similarly implied corporate name, unless and until such an entity is actually incorporated.

## 10. Risk Disclosure for Counterparties

Any party entering into an agreement with Vipto should be aware that, in the absence of incorporation, their contractual counterparty is the individual operating Vipto personally, and that any dispute or enforcement action would, as a matter of law, ordinarily be pursued against that individual rather than against a separate corporate entity. This disclosure is made in the interest of transparency and is not intended to discourage legitimate engagement, but to ensure counterparties contract with accurate expectations.

## 11. Responsibilities

The Owner is responsible for ensuring that any significant agreement entered into in Vipto's name is documented clearly, identifies the actual contracting party accurately, and does not imply a corporate structure that does not exist. Any individual granted limited contracting authority for a specific purpose is responsible for acting strictly within the scope of that authorization.

## 12. Limitations of This Document

This document explains the general legal position regarding contracting capacity in connection with Vipto. It does not constitute legal advice on the terms of any specific agreement, and any party negotiating a significant agreement with Vipto is encouraged to seek independent legal review of the specific terms and the identity of the contracting party before signing.
